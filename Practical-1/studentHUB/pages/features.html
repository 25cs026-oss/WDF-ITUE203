Features

StudentHub is designed to help students manage their academic work in one place. It provides simple portal features that make it easier to stay updated with classes, tasks, results, and important information.

Attendance Tracking

Students can view their attendance records for each subject. The portal can also show the total number of lectures attended and the overall attendance percentage.

This feature helps students understand whether their attendance is above or below the required limit.

Assignments

StudentHub allows students to check assignment titles, deadlines, and submission details. It helps students remember pending work and complete tasks on time.

This feature is useful for keeping academic work organized throughout the semester.

Results

Students can access internal marks, exam marks, grades, and semester performance details. The results section gives a clear summary of academic progress.

It can also help students review their performance in different subjects.

Timetable

The timetable feature provides information about weekly lectures, subjects, and class timings. Students can quickly check their schedule before attending classes.

This section helps in better time management and regular attendance.

Notices

Important updates and announcements from the college can be shown in the notices section. Students do not need to search in many places for academic information.

This feature keeps all important communication in one portal.

Resources

Students can access study materials, notes, reference files, and useful academic resources. This makes learning support available through the portal itself.

It is helpful for exam preparation, revision, and regular study.

User-Friendly Portal

StudentHub is planned as a simple and easy-to-use student portal. Its main purpose is to make academic information available in a structured and accessible way.

The portal can be useful for students, faculty members, and administrators in future development stages.
