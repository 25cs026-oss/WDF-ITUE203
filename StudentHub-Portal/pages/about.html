About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple academic management portal that keeps useful student information organized and easy to access.

What StudentHub Offers

Purpose

Provide one clear place for students to check daily academic information without searching across different platforms.

Main Features

Courses, attendance, assignments, results, timetables, notices, resources, faculty contacts, and college events.

Mission

Make academic information simple, accessible, responsive, and useful for every student.

Benefits for Students

  • Saves time by keeping academic information together.
  • Supports planning through timetables, notices, and due dates.
  • Works across mobile, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.
  • Provides quick access to faculty and support information.
